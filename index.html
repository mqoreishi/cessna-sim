How Guidance & State-Space Work Together

1. Guidance Block: Computes desired altitude hdes(S) and reference flight-path slope γref = −atan(2aS + b). Because the wings need an Angle of Attack (α) to generate lift, Guidance adds α to produce the nose pitch command θcmd = γcmd + α.

2. State-Space Dynamics: Models how elevator deflection δe creates aerodynamic pitching moment, changing pitch rate q, pitch attitude θ, angle of attack α, and airspeed perturbation u.
